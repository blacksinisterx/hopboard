Hopboard

Hop your clipboard between any two devices.

Scan a QR code and your phone and laptop share a clipboard, photos and files. No account, no app, and the server only ever sees gibberish.

Start a boardTakes about 5 seconds.
1

Scan

Open Hopboard on one device. Point the other one's camera at the QR code.

2

Allow

Check the matching emoji on both screens and tap Let it in. Nobody sneaks in with a stolen QR.

3

Hop

Type, paste or drop anything. It lands on the other device in a blink.
